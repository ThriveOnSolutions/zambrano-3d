Why piers only at N2

  • Piers cost more than footings, so they are used only where a footing cannot fit.
  • At N2 the gas strip is 5.9' behind the wall and the house is 2.5' to 4.4' in front. A 7' to 9' footing would hit one or the other.
  • E1 and E2 were moved off piers: open ground to the east takes a 7'-6" heel.
  • Result: 15 piers in the first option, 7 now. Taking the load off one tall wall let the spacing open from 6' to 7'.

What is left

  • City: every wall is under 4' and N2 uses a 3'-0" terrace, exactly as Ch. 9 asks. No variance, nothing discretionary.
  • Gas & easement: nothing inside either. Concrete stays 1'-0" off the gas strip, grading 6" off.
  • Groundwater: the first N2 pier hole is logged; casing is on standby and already priced. No separate boring.
  • Seal: a Texas P.E. seals the set. BSA (on the team) or A-1.
